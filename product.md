# Product brief

## Identity and purpose

**My Africa, My Narrative** — **Your story. Your voice. Our Africa.**

Primary track: Creation & Craft. Supporting tracks: Voices & Languages; Truth, Authorship & Ownership.

Help African creators turn original recordings, images and experiences into short documentaries they direct.
Help preserve voices, family histories, cultural knowledge and context with contributor permission and portable copies.
Original testimony records a person's account; linking it to a source does not independently verify historical truth.

## First audience

Emerging documentary makers, community storytellers and creative entrepreneurs working with phones.
Their first job: “Help me shape, shoot and finish this story using what I actually have.”

Pilot one tested language pair. Shona/English is the preferred candidate if real samples and fluent reviewers are available.
Mixed-language recordings need evaluation. The manual workflow remains usable when automated language quality is unsuitable.

## Four-stage creator journey

| Stage | Action | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Record | Record/upload narration, interviews, photos and clips. | Private playable sources with permission/attribution records. |
| Shape | Review words/timing, define intention, answer questions and edit scene cards. | A creator-approved story connected to original material. |
| Make | Declare resources and approve a shot plan/preview. | Achievable production and visible coverage gaps. |
| Export | Review captions, credits, sources and permissions. | Actual MP4/SRT and portable preservation copies. |

### Creator intention
Capture audience, meaning, tone, protected expressions, local context and privacy decisions.
Cultural context comes from the person/community telling the story.
AI proposes changes; the creator chooses and approves them.

### Focused interview
Ask a few useful questions about missing context, a turning point or an ending.
Let creators skip or answer later. Missing answers are not permission to invent facts.

### Scene editing
Show purpose, wording/origin, source links, visual selection, duration and review state.
Support reorder, edit, source playback and undo through revisions.
Protected quotations/terms survive generation and replanning.
Correcting caption text never synthesises new speech.
New spoken lines require a real recording and review in the MVP.

### Make it with what I have
Capture equipment, people/availability, locations, filming time, optional user budget and existing media.
Suggest shots using only those resources.
Show missing footage explicitly: a shot plan is not a completed scene.
Let the creator compare/apply/reject revised plans.
Do not invent local prices, access, permits, travel times or resources.

### Preservation and control
Keep original files and creator context. Record names/places/dates only when supplied.
Record contributor attribution and permission method honestly.
Support correction, withdrawal within the app and owner-authorised downloads.
Export originals, reviewed transcripts/timing, context, captions, credits and the film in a documented package.
This provides usable records and copies; it does not promise indefinite hosting or certify copyright.

## Demonstration scenario

A Zimbabwean designer records how her grandmother's tailoring influenced her streetwear.
She adds three family photographs and phone footage of her work.
She protects an original-language expression and approves an explanatory subtitle.
The app helps shape a film using one phone, one location and an afternoon.
An interview falls through; she reviews a revised plan using her original narration and existing materials.
She downloads the film and source package.

This is hypothetical until a consenting creator supplies actual material.
Synthetic history, quotations and images must never be presented as documentary evidence.

## Scope

Build one owner per private project, one original-voice documentary workflow and one vertical output format.
Local framework registration/login is enough for the pilot.
Support source timing correction, story/shot planning, actual rendering, reviewed language and preservation export.
Keep social feeds, marketplaces, payments, voice cloning and generated historical footage outside this build.

## Execution modes

| Mode | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Manual | Default no-key mode: real uploads, editing, timing, planning, review and rendering; no AI success claims. |
| Live | Actual configured provider requests; unavailable capabilities/errors are explicit. |
| Fixture | Explicitly selected deterministic test/demo outputs with a visible synthetic/fixture label. |

Editing, storage and rendering are real in each mode.
Synthetic tone/colour media proves processing only; it is not an authentic narrated documentary.

## Pilot evidence

Recruit three to five consenting creators when possible. Measure time to approved cut,
outside help required, intended meaning/phrase preservation, reviewed caption quality,
resource feasibility and willingness to share.
Report actual methods/results rather than invented time savings, endorsements or adoption.
